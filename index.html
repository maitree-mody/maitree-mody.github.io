03S1 · Wind Tunnel

Monetary policy research

How a repo rate change reaches housing credit

Barnard Laidlaw Scholars Program · May 2026 – present

−1.9%housing credit for every 1% rise in the policy rate

As one of 20 Barnard Laidlaw Scholars, I study how RBI rate changes pass through banks and credit markets into India's housing lending, with a faculty mentor. I built a 13-year panel of 36 states and 91 banks and ran fixed-effects regressions and a heterogeneity analysis in Stata. The effect is statistically significant mainly in less financially developed states. I've submitted the paper to the Laidlaw Foundation and will present it at Barnard's research symposium.

Union Bank of India, Mumbai

Banking & Economics Research Intern · Jun – Aug 2025

Working under the Chief Economic Advisor of India's fifth-largest public sector bank, I tracked the repo rate cycle against credit growth and GDP across 20+ years of data. My findings on policy transmission were credited in a published UBI report. Specialists in credit, FX and equities taught me to follow global markets and policy shifts.
